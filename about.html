About KL

What it is, what it isn't yet, and where it might go.

KL — Keane Language — started as a cipher: take a letter, replace it with the letter that follows plus itself, and see what falls out. Most of the alphabet follows that rule cleanly. A few letters don't, on purpose.

Right now KL has no grammar of its own — every KL sentence is really an English sentence routed through the table, word by word. What it becomes next is still open: new vocabulary, word order, maybe a script of its own.
